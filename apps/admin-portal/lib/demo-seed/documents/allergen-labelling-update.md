# Allergen labelling update

From: Marie Laurent, owner
To: all bakehouse and counter staff

Harbourside Bakery is a fictional business. This memo was written for a software demo.

## What's changing

From next Monday every product in the display cabinet needs an allergen card. The council's food safety officer asked for this at the last inspection.

## Allergen cards

- Each card lists the product name and every allergen it contains: gluten, wheat, milk, egg, soy, sesame, tree nuts and peanuts.
- Write "may contain traces of nuts" on every card. We use almonds in the bakehouse, so nothing is nut-free.
- The head baker writes the cards for new products before they go on sale.

## Labelling fillings

Trays of filling already carry the date they were made. From Monday they also carry an allergen sticker: orange for milk, yellow for egg, red for nuts.

## Customer questions

If a customer asks whether something is safe for an allergy, show them the card and the ingredient list in the folder under the till. Don't guess. If the answer isn't in the folder, say so and offer something that is labelled.

## Training

Everyone reads this memo and signs the sheet in the staff room by Friday. New starters cover allergens in their first week.
