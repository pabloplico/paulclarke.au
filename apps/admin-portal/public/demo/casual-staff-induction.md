# Casual staff induction

Harbourside Bakery is a fictional business. This file is sample data for a software demo.

Casual counter staff join us every summer. This is what to cover on their first shift.

## Before the first shift

- Send the roster for their first two weeks.
- Ask them to bring their food handler certificate, closed black shoes and a hat.

## First shift

1. Introductions, the staff room and where to put their things.
2. Fire exits, the first aid kit and who the first aid officer is.
3. Hand washing and when to change gloves.
4. How the till works, including refunds, which need the counter lead.
5. The display cabinet: what goes where and how to read the allergen cards.

## Shift swaps

Casuals can swap shifts with each other if the counter lead approves it by text at least 24 hours before. No-shows without notice are taken off the next roster.

## Pay

Pay runs fortnightly on Thursdays. Casual loading is included in the hourly rate.
