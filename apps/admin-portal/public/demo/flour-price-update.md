# Flour price update from Tableland Mills

Harbourside Bakery is a fictional business. This file is sample data for a software demo.

Tableland Mills has written to say that from the 1st of next month:

- Bakers flour goes from $28.50 to $31.00 per 25 kg bag.
- Wholemeal flour goes from $32.00 to $34.50 per bag.
- Rye flour is unchanged at $41.00 per bag.
- Orders of a full pallet (40 bags) get a 5% discount.
- The cut-off for next-day delivery moves from 2 pm to 12 pm.

## What we'll do

We currently order about 30 bags a week, split across Tuesday and Friday. Ordering a full pallet on Tuesdays only would get the discount and still keep four days of stock, but the flour store needs clearing to fit it.

The head baker will trial a single Tuesday pallet order for a month.
